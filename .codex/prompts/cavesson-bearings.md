# Re-read cavesson bearings

Run `cavesson reground` with your shell tool and present its output to the operator: the active feature + spec, the constitution principles in force, the sandbox-protected paths you must not write, the most recent progress entries, and the current cost posture.

Use this at the start of a session, after a long detour, or whenever you're unsure what the project's invariants or active work are. Treat the protected paths and principles as binding for the rest of the session.
